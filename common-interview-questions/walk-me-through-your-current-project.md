I work at a sports-tech company where players build a profile and join matches and tournaments. 

I recently built our face verification service — when someone signs up, we confirm it's a real person and that they're not already in the system before their profile goes live, which protects the platform from fake and duplicate accounts. I designed the full flow end-to-end, client and server side: the client does quick checks like live camera capture and face validation, then uploads the image and hands off to the backend, where the actual verification runs asynchronously through a queue and a background worker, so the user's never blocked waiting. I also built in failure handling and a self-healing safety net, since the flow touches several different services that can each fail independently. It's been running reliably in production, and the main thing I'm looking at next is better observability into that pipeline.
